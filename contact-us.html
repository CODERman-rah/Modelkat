About Us

Here at Modelkat, we strive to bring unique and hard-to-find models, toys, and collectibles to the Philippines. We understand how difficult it can be for collectors to find products that are not commonly available in local stores, especially those that come from overseas. That is why we aim to make these collectibles more accessible to everyone by offering a wide selection of figures and model kits from different brands. Above all, we are committed to providing our customers with affordable and honest prices, making collecting more enjoyable and accessible for everyone.
